Our Story

About the Mandal

A community built on devotion, culture and seva — for the neighbourhood, by the neighbourhood.

Celebrating Faith Since 2001 🙏

Harivilla Cha Raja has been a symbol of devotion, unity, and tradition since 2001, bringing families and devotees together to celebrate the blessings of Lord Ganesha.

Since 2023, the festival has been proudly organized by Shree Harihareshwar Mahadev Mitra Mandal, continuing this cherished legacy through cultural programs, community service, and grand Ganesh Utsav celebrations.
